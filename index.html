Our Process

Structured for clarity.

How we turn raw expert knowledge into institutional-grade investment signal.

Expert Selection

  • We source only operational experts and not market observers.
  • Someone who has a P&L impact in a similar company or ex leaders in the space.

Insight Formation

  • The expert writes a thorough note with information on a specific topic.
  • Standardized format: Context, Catalyst, and Implication.

Financial Translation

  • Our equity analysts work closely with experts to make the research piece financially suitable.
  • Direct impact mapped to revenue, margin structure, and key financial metrics.

Investment Context

  • The final note is checked by other experts to confirm the technicality and economically sound.
  • Published timely on Sapexa platform for institutional investors.
